场景：Harness 失败进化样本（主表=t_pd_wo，关联表=t_bc_encode_rule_group、t_lb_label_group、t_packing_rule）
用户问题：查询工单关联的编码规则组、标签组和包装规则
SQL：
SELECT t0.*, t1.*, t2.*, t3.* FROM t_pd_wo AS t0 LEFT JOIN t_bc_encode_rule_group AS t1 ON t0.encode_group_id = t1.id LEFT JOIN t_lb_label_group AS t2 ON t0.label_group_id = t2.id LEFT JOIN t_packing_rule AS t3 ON t0.pack_rule_id = t3.id;
